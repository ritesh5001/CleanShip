Iron ore and bauxite are heavy, dusty and colourful: grey-black and red-brown, in a hold that's supposed to end up clean enough for grain. The good news is that most mineral residue isn't oily or chemically sticky like petcoke. The bad news is that fine mineral dust gets into every corner and stains anything it sits on for long enough.

## What makes ore and bauxite residue awkward

- **Very fine dust,** especially from iron ore fines and pellets, that settles on every ledge and bracket
- **Staining:** red-brown from bauxite and many iron ores, which can be hard to distinguish from rust
- **Weight:** ore residue is heavy to lift out of a hold
- **Liquefaction risk** during carriage for some fines, which has nothing to do with cleaning but means wet residue may be present in bilges after discharge
- **Moisture:** wet ore and bauxite residue forms a mud that dries into a crust

## How do you remove iron ore dust?

1. **Get the stevedores to clear residue during discharge.** Iron ore is heavy and expensive to lift by hand.
2. **Sweep thoroughly,** top to bottom, and remove sweepings.
3. **Clean hatch covers and coamings.**
4. **Clear bilge wells,** which may hold ore mud.
5. **Wash down with seawater, top down.** Most iron ore dust washes off with water.
6. **Fresh water rinse** if the next cargo needs it.
7. **Dry.**

For ore-to-ore voyages, shovel clean or swept clean is often all that's required. Check the charterparty.

## Does iron ore require chemical cleaning?

Usually not for normal clean. For grain clean, it depends on staining. Most iron ore dust washes off with water and HP washing. If red or black staining remains and transfers onto a white cloth, a suitable cleaning chemical or a mineral stain remover followed by HP washing will usually shift it. Test first: some red marks are just rust.

## How do you clean bauxite stains?

Bauxite is the ore of aluminium and its fines leave a distinctive red-brown stain. To clean it:

1. Sweep and wash with seawater as normal
2. HP wash stained areas at close range
3. If staining remains and transfers to a white cloth, apply a stain remover suitable for mineral residue, observe the dwell time, and HP wash again
4. Fresh water rinse

Stains that don't transfer are often discoloured paint rather than residue. Ask the charterer how strict the load-port surveyor is likely to be.

## How do you remove mineral dust from upper frames?

The same way as any upper-frame residue: you need to get close. A combi-gun from the tank top removes the loose dust, but a layer often remains on the top faces of brackets and stiffeners. An HP lance at close range, via a cherry picker, staging or [rope access](/blog/cleaning-cargo-hold-upper-frames-without-scaffolding), is what clears it reliably.

## Iron ore to grain hold cleaning procedure

1. Maximise removal during discharge
2. Clean hatch covers, coamings and drain channels
3. Sweep thoroughly
4. Clear bilge wells of ore mud
5. Seawater wash, top down
6. HP wash, especially the upper structure, frames and brackets
7. Treat stubborn staining with a suitable chemical, then HP wash
8. White-cloth test at several heights
9. Fresh water rinse
10. Bilge wells cleaned, tested and covered as required
11. Dry and pre-inspect to grain standard

## Coal to iron ore, ore to coal

Changes between ore and coal are usually at the normal or shovel clean level, depending on the charterparty. Sweeping and removing residue may be all that's needed. If a grain cargo is likely on the following voyage, washing while the residue is fresh makes the later clean easier.

## Safety

- Ore dust is a respiratory hazard. Wear dust masks or respirators when sweeping
- Holds that carried iron ore, especially concentrates and direct reduced iron, may have oxygen-depleted atmospheres. Test before entry and follow the enclosed space procedure
- Wet ore mud in bilge wells is heavy and slippery; take care when removing it

## Frequently asked questions

### How do you remove iron ore dust from cargo holds?
Sweep thoroughly, clear bilge wells, wash down with seawater from the top and HP wash the upper structure at close range. Finish with a fresh water rinse if the next cargo requires it.

### Does iron ore require chemical cleaning?
Usually not for normal clean. For grain clean, chemicals may be needed if staining remains that transfers onto a white cloth.

### How do you clean bauxite stains from a cargo hold?
Wash and HP wash at close range, then apply a mineral stain remover to any staining that still transfers, followed by a second HP wash and fresh water rinse.

### How do you remove mineral dust from upper frames?
Get close with an HP lance via a cherry picker, staging or rope access. Washing from the tank top usually leaves dust on the top faces of brackets and stiffeners.

### What is the iron ore to grain cleaning procedure?
Remove residue during discharge, clean hatch covers, sweep, clear bilges, seawater wash, HP wash the upper structure, treat stains, rinse with fresh water, prepare bilges, dry and pre-inspect to grain standard.
